## Scope

Persuade mode for the public homepage of Ru AppStore (Vite multi-page site, `front/src/index.html`), with the same system carried to every other public page. Audience: iPhone owners in Russia who lost banking, marketplace, media and messaging apps removed from the App Store. Primary action: choose a package (1 / 6 / 12 months) and start registration at `/activate.html`. Proof: real catalog apps and icons, the live catalog size, the honest four-step flow with Apple waiting time, prices 590 / 1770 / 2360 ₽, «Мир» and СБП. No invented metrics or testimonials. Avoid overt state or patriotic imagery (no Kremlin, no map, no flag field). Quality bar set by the user: Yandex Plus / Т-Банк and Stripe.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app-store landing standard played straight at Т-Банк / Yandex Plus / Stripe craft: the offer, living proof from real catalog icons, and the packages within the first scroll. Refuses themed gimmick worlds and the generic SaaS feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Logo royal blue drenches the hero and pricing fields, deep navy gives depth, white sections carry navy ink; the only warm mark is a thin ribbon red used for savings. Heavy rounded display face echoing the wordmark over a clear text face. Big fully rounded white CTAs on blue, icon tiles with continuous corners, no glass cards, no bento.

STORY: The visitor sees familiar apps streaming back into an iPhone, grasps the four steps and the honest wait, compares terms in one switch, and starts registration.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed blue. Left five columns: kicker, H1 about apps that left the App Store returning to your iPhone, one line of support, white CTA «Выбрать тариф» and quiet «Как это работает», microline with the lowest monthly price. Right seven columns: WebGL stage, a white extruded logo "A" and a stylised iPhone, real app icons as 3D tiles orbiting and, on scroll, docking into the phone's home-screen grid; cursor tilts the stage.

FORM: category standard (canon), chosen by the user over the roll; seed 283f9ef0.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
