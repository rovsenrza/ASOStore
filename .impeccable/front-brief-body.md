Primary audience: an iPhone owner arriving in Safari. Primary action: start device setup. All user-facing copy is Russian; `[BRAND]` remains literal.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Device setup is one visible route, not a mysterious technical procedure. Refuse the generic consumer-tech hero with floating phone mockup and feature-card grid.

**OWN-WORLD:** A lit goldenrod timetable bed carries slate tinted registration slides, carbon rules, bible-ivory prose panes, vermilion pending marks, and bottle-green confirmations. One condensed timetable voice uses weight, reversal, and rules for hierarchy; controls are raked ticket cells rather than soft SaaS pills.

**STORY:** The visitor sees the whole route, understands why registration can wait, starts setup, and knows the native Storefront is the destination. Demonstration states are labeled as demo data; no commercial proof is invented.

Four oversized timetable slides cross the remaining viewport from lower-left to upper-right. The signature interaction moves one light along the rack as the user changes demo state.
