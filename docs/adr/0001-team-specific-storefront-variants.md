# Team-specific Ru AppStore variants on quota overflow

When an Apple team has no device slots left, the backend automatically assigns a new test device to the next active, authorized team with free capacity and a separately published Ru AppStore IPA bearing that team's distinct Bundle ID. Capacity is reserved transactionally and the assignment is audited. A team without a ready variant or approved bundle eligibility is never an automatic target; existing devices remain on their original team. This chooses per-team builds over rewriting Bundle IDs inside uploaded IPAs, preserving artifact identity and signing verification. Production activation depends on the product owner's stated Apple-specific written authorization covering the participating teams and distribution scope.
